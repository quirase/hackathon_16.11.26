Last weekend, I went to the countryside with two friends. We wanted to spend some time outside because the weather was warm and sunny. We travelled by train and arrived in a small village before lunch. First, we walked to a lake and took some photos. Then we had sandwiches and fruit near the water. In the afternoon, it started to rain, so we found a café and ordered hot drinks. We were a little tired when we got home, but we all had a great day.
