I started learning to cook when I moved into my own flat two years ago. At first, I only knew how to make a few simple meals, and I often ordered food because it was easier. However, I soon realised that cooking at home was cheaper and gave me more control over what I ate. I began watching short videos and trying one new recipe every week. Some meals didn’t turn out well, but I learned from my mistakes. Now I enjoy inviting friends over for dinner, and I’m much more confident in the kitchen.
