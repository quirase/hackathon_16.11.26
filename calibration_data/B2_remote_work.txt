Working from home has changed the way I organise my day. When I first started, I appreciated the flexibility, but I found it difficult to separate work from my personal life. I would often check messages late in the evening, even when nothing was urgent. Eventually, I decided to create a clearer routine: I set regular working hours, take a proper lunch break, and switch off notifications at the end of the day. These changes have helped me focus while I’m working and feel less stressed afterwards. Of course, remote work isn’t ideal for everyone, but a few practical boundaries can make a significant difference.
