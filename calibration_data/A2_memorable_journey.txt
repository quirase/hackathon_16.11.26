Last summer, I travelled to another city with my sister. We wanted to visit our cousin, who had moved there a few months earlier. We bought train tickets online and left early on Saturday morning. The journey took about four hours, so we brought some food, water, and books with us.

When we arrived, our cousin met us at the station. First, we went to her flat and left our bags there. Then she showed us the city centre. We walked through a large park, visited an old museum, and had lunch at a small café. I ordered a vegetable soup and a sandwich. The food was simple, but it was very tasty.

On Sunday, we wanted to go to the beach, but the weather changed. It became cold and started to rain. Instead, we stayed inside and watched an old comedy film. In the evening, our cousin cooked dinner, and we talked about our childhood.

We returned home on Monday morning. I was tired after the trip, but I felt happy because we had spent a lovely weekend together. I would like to visit that city again, perhaps when the weather is warmer.
