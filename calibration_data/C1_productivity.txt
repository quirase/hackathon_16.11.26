One of the most useful lessons I’ve learned at work is that being busy and being productive are not necessarily the same thing. For a long time, I measured my progress by the number of tasks I completed, without considering whether those tasks actually moved a project forward. Over time, I began setting aside space to review priorities and question commitments that no longer served a clear purpose. This approach can feel uncomfortable, particularly in environments where immediate responses are expected. Nevertheless, I’ve found that a little reflection often prevents far more time from being wasted later, and it leads to decisions I can explain with greater confidence.
